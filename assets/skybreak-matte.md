# Skybreak panorama

## Generation prompt

Create a seamless 2:1 equirectangular skybox environment texture for Skybreak, a third-person 3D platform game set in an abandoned industrial quarry above a sea of clouds. Complete 360-degree panorama, left and right edges must wrap seamlessly. Stylised premium game environment matte painted as a restrained realistic 3D landscape, dark teal slate-blue alpine ridges in the distance, luminous cloud sea below, blue-green clear sky above, golden sunrise from upper left, atmospheric depth, mineral greys and hints of warm copper. Horizon centred vertically. Near-ground area is entirely soft cloud with no solid features. Distant mountains are low and along the horizon with clear quiet negative space for legible real-time foreground platforms. Subtle skies, rich detailed cloud forms, natural light. No text, people, foreground buildings or objects, no UI, no borders.
