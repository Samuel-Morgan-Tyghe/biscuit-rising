# Skybreak materials

The built-in imagegen tool generated these tileable colour textures. The game repeats them in world units on the cliff and platform geometry. It also uses their luminance for surface bump detail.

## Slate

[slate.jpg](slate.jpg)

Create a seamless square physically based ALBEDO material texture for a large sheer alpine quarry cliff, for a premium realistic 3D video game. Straight-on flat scanned surface of naturally fractured dark slate and granodiorite, hard jagged carved stone strata, thin subtle pale mineral seams and an occasional desaturated ochre mineral deposit. Strong fine-scale tonal and geological detail, blue charcoal neutral. Even diffuse cross-polarized light with ZERO shadows, ambient occlusion or directional shading, no depth perspective, no backdrop or objects, no text. Completely full bleed with genuine repeatable seamless opposite edges in X and Y. No obvious giant feature that makes the repeat visible. Texture for a broad mountain vertical surface, no moss, no snow.

## Steel

[steel.jpg](steel.jpg)

Create a seamless square physically based ALBEDO texture of industrial steel diamond tread plate for a premium realistic 3D game on an alpine quarry gantry. Orthographic flat material scan filling the entire image, even diffuse cross-polarized illumination, worn dark cool blue-grey galvanized steel with a uniform restrained small raised diamond lozenge repeat, mild scratches and abrasion at a few points, no heavy orange except tiny rust flecks. Exactly no cast shadows, no directional highlights, no objects, text or stripes, no fisheye. Realistic material. Genuine edge-to-edge seamless repeat in both axes. Consistent scale across frame, oriented square and full bleed.

## Timber

[timber.jpg](timber.jpg)

Square seamless photogrammetric albedo texture for an outdoor mountain footbridge in a realistic 3D video game. Macro straight-on orthographic scan of silvered, worn larch heartwood with long parallel vertical grain and very fine dark splits, subtle brown and cool gray variations, a few discreet darker knots typical of old alpine timber. One continuous timber surface with no separate boards, nails or screws, no strong edge, no shadows, no perspective, no text or objects. Flat cross-polarized diffuse illumination, physically plausible moderate albedo. Repeatable opposite edges both axes; dense detailed grain for VR/game rendering, natural and weathered but structurally sound. Full bleed.

## Brick

[brick.jpg](brick.jpg)

Seamless square colour texture for historic industrial masonry on a mountain quarry in a realistic videogame. A full bleed orthographic albedo scan of weathered hand-fired burnt sienna and deep brown clay bricks in a medium-scale running bond, very fine gray putty mortar, chipped corners, understated soot and grey mineral deposits, each brick slightly varied, real masonry, tightly laid. Flat and evenly lit with no hard light, perspective, objects, text or borders. Exactly seamless repeat along top/bottom and left/right, brick rows align across the borders. Neutral vividness, photogrammetry-quality surface detail.
